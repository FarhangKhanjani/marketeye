Marketeye · Phase 1 report

A crypto screener that ranks altcoins against Bitcoin

Phase 1 is the data foundation of a signal service. Every run pulls the market priced in BTC, drops anything that isn't a tradable altcoin, and sorts what's left into long candidates, short candidates and outliers. It also tracks Bitcoin's dominance, which will later decide whether to favour BTC or alts.

How one run works

Each step below shows the live count from the snapshot used for this report. A full run costs 3 API calls out of a 10,000-per-day allowance.

Where the altcoins landed

Each dot is one coin, placed by how much it moved against BTC in the last 24 hours. Bigger dots trade more volume. The shaded bands are the long and short entry zones. Coins far outside both are big pumps or dumps, which usually don't offer clean entries.

Bitcoin dominance, last 30 days

Dominance is Bitcoin's share of the total crypto market cap. When it rises, money is flowing into BTC; when it falls, alts tend to outperform. Phase 1 only collects it, and the trend rules come next.

The regime map

The plan is to classify Bitcoin's trend and dominance's trend, then pick a playbook from this grid. The grid is already in the code; the numeric rules that decide each trend are not defined yet.
